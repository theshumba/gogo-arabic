# Plan 67-02: Zone-Entry Micro-Reviews

## Context

When a player enters a new zone, they should get a quick 2-3 word review of FSRS-due words. This reinforces vocabulary in context without interrupting gameplay for long.

## Implementation

### Step 1: Add MICRO_REVIEW event to eventBusTypes.js

```javascript
MICRO_REVIEW_TRIGGER: 'react:quiz:micro-review-trigger',
MICRO_REVIEW_CLOSE: 'react:quiz:micro-review-close',
```

### Step 2: Create MicroReviewOverlay component

Compact overlay (bottom third of screen) showing one word at a time from 2-3 due words. Simple ar-to-en multiple choice. Auto-closes after all words reviewed.

### Step 3: Wire zone change to micro-review trigger

In useZoneEvents.js `handleZoneChange`, after existing logic:
1. Get FSRS due cards from Redux state
2. If >= 2 due cards exist and cooldown elapsed, pick 2-3 random due words
3. Emit MICRO_REVIEW_TRIGGER with word IDs
4. Freeze player

### Step 4: Add MicroReviewOverlay to GameLayout

Lazy-load MicroReviewOverlay, listen for MICRO_REVIEW_TRIGGER event, show overlay with due words.
