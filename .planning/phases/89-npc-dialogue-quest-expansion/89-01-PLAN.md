# Phase 89 — Expanded NPC Dialogue & Quest Storylines

## Overview
Massively expanded narrative content for the Gogo Arabic RPG. Adds deep backstories for 24 faction NPCs, a 20-quest main storyline across 5 chapters and all 8 zones, 8 multi-part side quest chains (24 side quests total), and friendship-gated dialogue trees for all 24 NPCs. Every piece of content naturally introduces Arabic vocabulary through narrative.

## Files Created

### Data
- `src/data/npcBackstories.js` — Deep backstories for 24 faction NPCs (4 per faction), including motivations, secrets, relationship quotes (4 tiers), teaching style, favorite topics
- `src/data/mainQuestline.js` — 20-quest main storyline across 5 chapters, threading through all 8 zones with vocabulary introduction per quest
- `src/data/sideQuestChains.js` — 8 side quest chains (1 per zone, 3 quests each = 24 total), with zone-specific stories and vocabulary
- `src/data/npcDialogueExpansion.js` — Friendship-gated dialogue trees for 24 NPCs (3 tiers: friendly/close/secret at 50/75/90 friendship)

### Tests
- `src/data/__tests__/npcBackstories.test.js` — 24 NPCs covered, required fields, Arabic text validation, faction mapping
- `src/data/__tests__/mainQuestline.test.js` — 20 quests, 5 chapters, prerequisite chain validation, vocabulary refs
- `src/data/__tests__/sideQuestChains.test.js` — 8 chains, 24 quests, unique IDs, prerequisite validation, zone coverage
- `src/data/__tests__/npcDialogueExpansion.test.js` — All 24 NPCs have dialogues, friendship triggers valid, Arabic text present

## Content Summary

### NPC Backstories (24 NPCs, 6 factions)
- **Scholars**: Scholar Yusuf, Librarian Ibrahim, Scribe Amina, Astronomer Zain
- **Merchants**: Merchant Fatima, Trader Hassan, Spice Seller Layla, Carpet Seller Jamal
- **Artisans**: Blacksmith Daud, Weaver Zahra, Baker Yasmin, Herbalist Maryam
- **Travelers**: Guide Amira, Wanderer Ali, Captain Rashid, Guide Salim
- **Guardians**: Guard Hamza, Vizier Abbas, Dockmaster Nadia, Mountain Hermit Idris
- **Artists**: Storyteller Noor, Poet Rumi, Princess Aisha, Garden Keeper Leila

### Main Questline (20 quests, 5 chapters)
- **Ch1 "The Newcomer"**: Arrival, meet mentors, learn greetings and alphabet (oasis-village, ancient-library)
- **Ch2 "The Marketplace"**: Trade, cultural immersion, food and direction words (desert-marketplace, bedouin-camp)
- **Ch3 "The Court"**: Politics, formal Arabic, nature vocabulary (royal-palace, mountain-pass)
- **Ch4 "The Voyage"**: Sea navigation, discovery, body and health words (coastal-port, hidden-oasis)
- **Ch5 "The Scholar"**: Mastery, gathering words of power, final ceremony (all zones)

### Side Quest Chains (8 chains, 24 quests)
1. Oasis Village — The Mystery of the Dry Well (water/nature vocab)
2. Ancient Library — The Lost Catalogue (number/reading vocab)
3. Desert Marketplace — The Counterfeit Coins (trade/clothing vocab)
4. Bedouin Camp — The Lost Song (adjective/emotion vocab)
5. Royal Palace — The Vizier's Dilemma (formal/family vocab)
6. Mountain Pass — The Eagle's Nest (animal/direction vocab)
7. Coastal Port — The Ghost Ship (sea/emotion/family vocab)
8. Hidden Oasis — The Seed of Knowledge (nature/wisdom vocab)

### Dialogue Expansion (72 dialogues across 24 NPCs)
- Each NPC has 3 tiers of unlockable dialogue (friendship 50/75/90)
- Each dialogue teaches a vocabulary word through narrative
- Dialogues reveal character depth, secrets, and interconnected storylines

## Design Decisions
- All files are self-contained — no edits to existing files
- Arabic text is authentic and culturally appropriate
- NPC personalities are consistent with their existing npcs-meta.json data
- Quest prerequisite chains are strictly sequential (no circular dependencies)
- Vocabulary is introduced naturally through dialogue, never as isolated drills
- Backstory secrets create hooks for future quest content
- Interconnected NPC relationships (e.g., Rumi loves Leila, Ali and Amira were engaged, Nadia knows where Rashid's daughter is) create a living world
- The main questline's "manuscript" thread provides narrative momentum across all 8 zones
- Side quests explore zone-specific stories without requiring main quest progress
- Friendship dialogue tiers reward relationship-building with deeper narrative access
