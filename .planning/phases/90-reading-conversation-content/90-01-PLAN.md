# Phase 90 — Additional Reading & Conversation Content

## Goal
Expand the Arabic learning content library with 40 new reading passages, 24 new conversation scenarios, and 20 themed vocabulary sets.

## Deliverables

### 1. Reading Passages Expanded (`src/data/readingPassagesExpanded.js`)
- 40 passages (10 per CEFR level: A1, A2, B1, B2)
- Topics NOT covered in the original 60 passages
- IDs prefixed with `exp_rp_` to avoid collisions
- Export: `READING_PASSAGES_EXPANDED`

### 2. Conversation Scenarios Expanded (`src/data/conversationScenariosExpanded.js`)
- 24 scenarios (3 per zone, 8 zones)
- CEFR distribution per zone: 1 A2, 1 B1, 1 B2
- IDs prefixed with `exp_` to avoid collisions
- Export: `CONVERSATION_SCENARIOS_EXPANDED`

### 3. Themed Vocabulary Sets (`src/data/themedVocabSets.js`)
- 20 curated vocabulary groups
- 15-20 words per set with fun facts
- IDs use `_set` suffix
- Export: `THEMED_VOCAB_SETS`

### 4. Tests
- `src/data/__tests__/readingPassagesExpanded.test.js`
- `src/data/__tests__/conversationScenariosExpanded.test.js`
- `src/data/__tests__/themedVocabSets.test.js`

## ID Scheme
- Reading passages: `exp_rp_a1_001` through `exp_rp_b2_010`
- Conversation scenarios: `exp_{zone-prefix}_{topic}_{number}`
- Vocab sets: `{topic}_set`

## No Edits to Existing Files
All content is in new files only. No modifications to readingPassages.js or conversationScenarios.js.
