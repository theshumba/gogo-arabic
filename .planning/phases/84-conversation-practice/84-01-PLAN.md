# Phase 84 — Conversation Practice Mode (CONV-01 + CONV-02)

## Overview
Structured conversation practice where players construct Arabic sentences from word banks.
40 scenarios (5 per zone), chat-style UI with NPC dialogue, drag/tap word tiles, scoring.

## Key Decisions
- **Word bank with distractors**: 2-3 extra plausible-but-wrong Arabic words per exchange
- **RTL sentence builder**: tiles placed right-to-left matching Arabic reading direction
- **Chat-style UI**: NPC messages left-aligned (blue), player messages right-aligned (gold)
- **Scoring**: exact match = 100%, 1 word off = 75%, 2+ off = 50%, wrong = 25%
- **CEFR distribution per zone**: 2 A1, 1 A2, 1 B1, 1 B2
- **Default exports** on components for lazy loading compatibility
- **NPC references**: uses existing NPC names/portraits from npcs-meta.json

## Scenario Topics by Zone
- **oasis-village**: greetings, introductions, asking for water, family, daily routine
- **ancient-library**: asking about books, knowledge, requesting help, describing search, thanking scholar
- **desert-marketplace**: negotiating prices, products, ordering food, directions, quantities
- **bedouin-camp**: hospitality, weather, sharing stories, traditions, describing desert
- **royal-palace**: formal greetings, requesting audience, describing quest, gratitude, farewell
- **mountain-pass**: directions, danger warnings, terrain, offering help, travel plans
- **coastal-port**: ships, destinations, sea weather, trading goods, farewell before voyage
- **hidden-oasis**: expressing wonder, history, beauty, discoveries, philosophical reflection

## Architecture
- ConversationScenarios is a pure data module (no Redux dependency)
- WordBank is a reusable component (receives words + callbacks, no Redux)
- ConversationPracticeOverlay wraps WordBank with Redux-connected state management
- conversationSlice tracks per-scenario scores, attempts, stats per zone
