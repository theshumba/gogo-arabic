  <action>
  **PostBattleReview.jsx**: Detailed Arabic usage review after each battle.

  This replaces/enhances the existing BattleResult.jsx stats section. Shown after the victory/defeat screen when player clicks "Review Arabic" button.

  Props: `{ battleData, onClose }` where battleData is from arabicUsedThisBattle and battle history.

  Sections:

  **1. Overall Stats** (top of review):
  - Total Arabic words used (Arabic numeral)
  - Overall accuracy percentage (Arabic numeral + English %)
  - Battle duration (Arabic numeral minutes:seconds)
  - Max combo streak (Arabic numeral)
  - Status effects applied (count, Arabic numeral)

  **2. Vocabulary Table** (main section):
  - Scrollable table of all words used in this battle
  - Columns: Arabic word (Amiri font), English translation, Accuracy (color-coded: green >=80%, yellow 50-79%, red <50%), Times used
  - Sort by accuracy ascending (weakest words first — prioritize learning)
  - Words that were incorrect get a "📝 Review" tag indicating they'll appear in FSRS review

  **3. Grammar Combos Attempted** (if any):
  - List of grammar combos attempted during battle
  - Each shows: combo type (إضافة / تصريف / جملة), Arabic input, result (success/fail), damage multiplier achieved
  - Failed combos show correct answer for learning

  **4. Status Effects Encountered**:
  - List of status effects applied (to player and enemy)
  - Each shows: Arabic name, English name, who it affected, turns active

  **5. "Practice Weak Words" CTA button**:
  - If any words had accuracy < 80%, show button that opens vocabulary review session
  - Emits EVENTS.REVIEW_SESSION_OPEN with weak word IDs

  Layout: Full-screen overlay (similar to BattleResult), scrollable content, gold theme.
  All numbers displayed in Arabic numerals using toArabicNumerals().
  RTL direction throughout.

  **PostBattleReview.module.css**:
  - `.reviewScreen` — full overlay, dark background, scroll container
  - `.statsGrid` — 2x2 grid for overall stats
  - `.statBox` — stat with Arabic numeral large, label below
  - `.vocabTable` — full-width table, alternating row colors
  - `.accuracyBadge` — colored badge (green/yellow/red)
  - `.comboSection` — list of combos with success/fail indicators
  - `.effectSection` — list of status effects
  - `.practiceButton` — gold button CTA at bottom
  - `.closeButton` — top-right close button
  </action>
  <verify>
  - `npx vite build` succeeds
  - PostBattleReview.jsx and PostBattleReview.module.css exist
  </verify>
