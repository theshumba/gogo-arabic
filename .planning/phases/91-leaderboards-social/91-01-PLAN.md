# Phase 91 — Leaderboards & Social Features

## Goal
Add local leaderboards and shareable stats for family/shared-device competition. No backend required — all data persists in localStorage.

## Deliverables

### 1. Multi-Profile Leaderboard Service (`src/services/multiProfileLeaderboardService.js`)
- localStorage-based leaderboard with 8 categories (XP, words learned, words mastered, streak, perfect quizzes, achievements, reading passages, writing best)
- Per-player personal bests with case-insensitive name matching
- Up to 50 entries per category, sorted descending by score
- Profile management: create/delete/load profiles (max 5 per device)
- Exports: `LEADERBOARD_CATEGORIES`, `saveScore`, `getLeaderboard`, `getPlayerRank`, `getPlayerBests`, `clearLeaderboards`, `loadProfiles`, `createProfile`, `deleteProfile`, `getMaxProfiles`

### 2. Shareable Stats Generator (`src/services/shareableStats.js`)
- `generateShareText` — share text with hashtags (#GogoArabic, #LearnArabic, Arabic hashtag)
- `generateStatsSummary` — structured stats with CEFR estimation and encouraging fun facts
- `generateWeeklyReport` — weekly progress summary with encouragement
- `formatStudyTime` — human-readable duration formatting
- Pure functions, no side effects

### 3. Leaderboard Slice (`src/store/slices/leaderboardSlice.js`)
- State: `playerName`, `profiles[]`, `activeProfile`, `lastSynced`
- Reducers: `setPlayerName`, `createProfile`, `switchProfile`, `removeProfile`, `syncScores`, `hydrateProfiles`
- Selectors: `selectPlayerName`, `selectProfiles`, `selectActiveProfile`, `selectActiveProfileId`, `selectLastSynced`, `selectProfileCount`, `selectCanCreateProfile`
- Not yet registered in store.js (requires Phase 91 integration step)

### 4. Leaderboard Panel (`src/components/Social/LeaderboardPanel.jsx` + `.module.css`)
- Full panel with 8 category tabs (scrollable), top 10 list, rank badges (gold/silver/bronze)
- Current player highlighted in gold
- Player rank card when outside top 10
- Weekly report section with days active, XP, words, time
- Share My Stats button + optional Profiles button
- Dark theme: #1a1a2e background, #FFD700 gold accents

### 5. Stats Share Card (`src/components/Social/StatsShareCard.jsx` + `.module.css`)
- CEFR level badge, 6-stat grid, earned badges, fun facts
- Share (Web Share API) + Copy Text (clipboard) + Close actions
- Arabic text with RTL formatting
- Expanded version of existing CEFR SocialShareCard

### 6. Profile Manager (`src/components/Social/ProfileManager.jsx` + `.module.css`)
- Create profiles (name + emoji avatar picker, 8 options)
- Switch profiles, delete inactive profiles
- Max 5 profiles, duplicate name prevention
- Synced between localStorage service and Redux slice

### 7. Tests
- `src/services/__tests__/multiProfileLeaderboardService.test.js` — 28 tests: save/get/rank, multiple categories, storage persistence, profile CRUD
- `src/services/__tests__/shareableStats.test.js` — 25 tests: text generation, stats summary, weekly report, time formatting
- `src/store/slices/__tests__/leaderboardSlice.test.js` — 28 tests: profile management, syncing, selectors
- `src/components/Social/__tests__/LeaderboardPanel.test.jsx` — 13 tests: rendering, category switching, highlighting, callbacks
