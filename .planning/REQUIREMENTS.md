# Requirements: GoGo Arabic v6.1

**Defined:** 2026-02-13
**Core Value:** Players naturally learn Arabic through guided exploration and interaction in an engaging RPG world — never wondering "what should I do next?" or "how do I practice?"

## v6.1 Requirements

### Crafting Professions

- [ ] **CRAFT-01**: Player can learn 6 crafting professions (Calligrapher/خطاط, Cook/طباخ, Blacksmith/حداد, Herbalist/عطار, Weaver/نساج, Builder/بناء)
- [ ] **CRAFT-02**: Each profession has 10 skill levels with increasingly complex Arabic requirements
- [ ] **CRAFT-03**: Player can discover and collect recipes through exploration, NPC teaching, and experimentation
- [ ] **CRAFT-04**: Player can view recipes in a RecipeBook UI showing Arabic ingredient names (ingredient usable only if vocabulary word is learned)
- [ ] **CRAFT-05**: Each profession has a unique crafting mini-game (calligraphy tracing, recipe-order following, rhythm counting, plant identification, pattern matching, directional placement)
- [ ] **CRAFT-06**: Crafted item quality scales with Arabic accuracy during the crafting mini-game
- [ ] **CRAFT-07**: Each profession teaches ~50 domain-specific Arabic vocabulary words integrated with FSRS

### Resource Gathering

- [ ] **RSRC-01**: Player can gather zone-specific resources with Arabic names from gathering spots in the world
- [ ] **RSRC-02**: Resource quality scales with tool level and gathering skill
- [ ] **RSRC-03**: Gathering spots respawn on a timed schedule encouraging daily play

### Crafting Integration

- [ ] **CINT-01**: Crafted equipment integrates with existing equipment system (crafted items can be best-in-slot)
- [ ] **CINT-02**: Crafted consumables (food, potions) provide battle buffs
- [ ] **CINT-03**: Profession mastery contributes to zone reputation and unlocks NPC dialogue
- [ ] **CINT-04**: Calligraphy profession creates enchantment scrolls that enhance equipment with Arabic inscriptions

### Status Effects

- [ ] **STAT-01**: Battle system supports 20+ status effects with Arabic names (حيرة/Confusion, قوة/Strength, سرعة/Speed, حماية/Protection, سم/Poison, صمت/Silence, عمى/Blindness, شجاعة/Courage, حكمة/Wisdom, بركة/Blessing, etc.)
- [ ] **STAT-02**: Applying a status effect in battle requires knowing the Arabic vocabulary word
- [ ] **STAT-03**: Status effect vocabulary is auto-added to FSRS review queue when encountered
- [ ] **STAT-04**: Status effect combinations create compound effects (e.g., سرعة + قوة = devastating attack)

### Grammar Combos

- [ ] **COMBO-01**: Player can execute noun+adjective combos (إضافة) by describing targets accurately for bonus damage
- [ ] **COMBO-02**: Player can chain verb conjugations across forms for escalating damage
- [ ] **COMBO-03**: Player can construct complete Arabic sentences for ultimate attacks
- [ ] **COMBO-04**: Combo meter builds with consecutive correct Arabic answers and displays with Arabic numerals
- [ ] **COMBO-05**: Enemies can break combos by forcing tense switches or root changes

### Advanced Battle Mechanics

- [ ] **ADVB-01**: Battle system supports multi-target encounters (up to 4 enemies)
- [ ] **ADVB-02**: Front/back row positioning affects risks and rewards in battle
- [ ] **ADVB-03**: Battle items (potions, scrolls, food from crafting) are usable during combat
- [ ] **ADVB-04**: Post-battle review screen shows all Arabic used with accuracy statistics
- [ ] **ADVB-05**: Retreat mechanic requires correctly answering an Arabic question to flee

### Arena & Challenges

- [ ] **ARENA-01**: Player can enter a wave-based survival arena with increasing Arabic difficulty
- [ ] **ARENA-02**: Player can attempt boss rush mode (all bosses with story interludes)
- [ ] **ARENA-03**: Puzzle battles require specific Arabic knowledge (not brute-forceable)
- [ ] **ARENA-04**: Arena has a leaderboard tracking player performance and streaks

## Future Requirements

### Crafting (v7.0+)

- **CRAFT-F01**: Auction house / trade caravan system between zones
- **CRAFT-F02**: Seasonal/weather-gated resource gathering
- **CRAFT-F03**: Custom Arabic naming for crafted items (player types Arabic name)

### Combat (v7.0+)

- **ADVB-F01**: PvE tournaments with seasonal themes
- **ADVB-F02**: Battle replays stored for learning review
- **ADVB-F03**: Weather/time affecting battle conditions

## Out of Scope

| Feature | Reason |
|---------|--------|
| PvP multiplayer | Single-player focus, high complexity |
| Real-time crafting timers | Frustrating, anti-educational |
| Loot boxes / gacha | Predatory, undermines educational trust |
| Pay-to-win crafting shortcuts | Inappropriate for education |
| Voice-based spell casting | Infrastructure not ready |
| Crafting automation / idle mechanics | Undermines active Arabic learning |
| Player housing / base building | Defer to v9.0 (Phase 50) |
