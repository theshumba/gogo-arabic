### Crafting (Phase 31)
- 6 crafting professions (خطاط/Calligrapher, طباخ/Cook, حداد/Blacksmith, عطار/Herbalist, نساج/Weaver, بناء/Builder)
- 100 recipes with vocabulary-gated Arabic ingredients

- Multi-target battles (up to 4 enemies, front/back row positioning)
- Wave-based arena (ArenaController + ArenaHUD)
- Boss rush mode (6 bosses with story interludes)
- 3 puzzle battle types (Arabic knowledge-gated)
- Post-battle Arabic vocabulary review with accuracy statistics
- Arena leaderboard with ranked performance tracking
- Arabic flee mechanic (accuracy >= 0.8 required)
