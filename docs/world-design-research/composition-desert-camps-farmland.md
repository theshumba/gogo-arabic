# Composition Rules — Desert Settlements, Nomad Camps, Farmland

Phase 0 reference analysis for the Gogo Arabic full world rebuild.
Source images: `/Users/theshumba/Desktop/Gogo-World-References/` categories 02 (desert-oasis-villages), 05 (camps-nomad), 09 (farmland).
All tile counts below are **gameplay tiles** (our 64px grid, one tile = one walkable cell), matching how the reference games use their own grids. Rules are written to be followed literally by map-building agents. Zones affected: `oasis_village`, `desert_marketplace`, `bedouin_camp`, `farmland` (ground rules also apply to every outdoor desert zone).

---

## 1. Making open sand read as DESIGNED space

1. **Frame every outdoor map with a hard boundary, never raw sand edge.** Suhalla uses purple rock cliffs, Lalivero uses a sandstone wall, Kakkara uses a dense palm-forest ring 4–8 tiles thick, FF6 Imperial Camp uses cliff/forest. (`golden-sun-suhalla-gba-map.webp`, `golden-sun-lalivero-gba-map.webp`, `secret-of-mana-kakkara-snes-map.webp`, `ff6-imperial-camp-tents-fullmap.png`). Kenmi: use dune-cliff / rock tiles or dense palm autotile as a 2–6 tile border band; exits are 2–3 tile cuts through it.
2. **Carve the playable clearing as an organic blob, not a rectangle.** Kakkara's sand clearing has stepped, irregular edges against the palm ring; Suhalla's cliffs meander. Vary the border band thickness by 2–4 tiles every 4–8 tiles of length. (`secret-of-mana-kakkara-snes-map.webp`, `golden-sun-suhalla-gba-map.webp`).
3. **Two sand values minimum: base sand + trampled/dark sand for circulation.** Naribwe marks its plaza and paths purely with darker trampled-dirt patches; Kakkara uses a gravel/pebble path strip between hut doors. No formal road tiles needed in villages — a 2–3 tile wide trampled strip connecting all doors reads as "street". (`golden-sun-lost-age-naribwe-gba-map.webp`, `secret-of-mana-kakkara-snes-map.webp`).
4. **Prop-cluster rhythm: a point of interest every 8–15 tiles of open sand.** EarthBound's desert stays readable because pyramids, ponds, single palms, and cacti punctuate the dunes; Suhalla scatters pot/jar clusters. In open sand place one small cluster (2–4 jars, a palm, rocks, bones, cactus) every 8–15 tiles; never leave a 20×20 area empty. (`earthbound-scaraba-snes-map.webp`, `golden-sun-suhalla-gba-map.webp`).
5. **Props cluster at doors and corners, in groups of 2–4, never singly-spaced grids.** Suhalla and Naribwe pile clay jars beside doorways and at building corners (2–4 per pile, touching or 1 tile apart). Kenmi: use jar/pot/crate props in touching clumps; leave 1 tile of clear sand around the clump so it doesn't block circulation. (`golden-sun-suhalla-gba-map.webp`, `golden-sun-lost-age-naribwe-gba-map.webp`).
6. **Ground-texture noise ≈ 1 decal per 4×4 area.** HM GB and Stardew keep big open areas alive with pebbles, tufts, cracks, stumps at roughly one small non-blocking decal per 4×4 tile patch, denser near edges, sparser in walk corridors. (`harvestmoon-gb-ranch-fullmap.png`, `stardew-standard-farm-fullmap.png`).

## 2. Oasis / water placement — the anchor

7. **Water is the composition anchor: place the pond at map center or top-center, and orient everything at it.** Kakkara: pond dead-center, 3 huts in a triangle around it. Burning Heroes: rectangular pool center, tents ringed around. Arabian Nights Oasis: pond top-center with the whole map flowing south from it. (`secret-of-mana-kakkara-snes-map.webp`, `burning-heroes-oasis-fullmap.png`, `arabian-nights-oasis-fullmap.png`).
8. **Village pond size: 4×3 to 8×6 tiles; wilderness oasis lake: up to 14×10.** Kakkara's pond is ~5×4; Scaraba's in-town ponds are 2×2–3×2 accents; Arabian Nights' oasis lake is roughly a third of the map width. Bigger than that becomes a lake zone, not an oasis. (`secret-of-mana-kakkara-snes-map.webp`, `earthbound-scaraba-snes-map.webp`, `arabian-nights-oasis-fullmap.png`).
9. **Ring water with a 1–2 tile grass/reed halo, then sand.** Every oasis reference (Arabian Nights, Wozz, DQ5) puts a grass ring around the pond and lets grass patches trail off in blobs — vegetation density decays with distance from water. Kenmi: grass autotile halo 1–2 tiles, then 2–4 detached grass blobs (3–6 tiles each) within 4 tiles of shore, then bare sand. (`arabian-nights-oasis-fullmap.png`, `wozz-oasis-fullmap.png`).
10. **Give one shore a hard edge, the rest soft.** Arabian Nights' pond has a rock lip along its north shore only; DQ3 Isis rings the castle moat with stone on the approach side. One rocky/built edge (often the north/back side) makes the water read intentional. (`arabian-nights-oasis-fullmap.png`, `dragon-warrior-iii-isis-castle-town-nes-map.webp`).
11. **Doors face the water.** Around an oasis, every building/tent entrance points toward the pond; the pond edge doubles as the plaza. Kaipo (FF4) threads water channels *between* buildings so the water is always beside the walking path. (`secret-of-mana-kakkara-snes-map.webp`, `burning-heroes-oasis-fullmap.png`, `ff4-oasis-village-kaipo-fullmap.png`).
12. **Wells substitute for ponds in dry plazas.** Naribwe and HM SNES put a well 2–4 tiles off-center in the main open space when there's no pond — never centered exactly, never against a wall. (`golden-sun-lost-age-naribwe-gba-map.webp`, `harvestmoon-snes-farm-spring-fullmap.png`).

## 3. Palm clustering

13. **Palms cluster in 2–4 near water, stand alone in open sand.** Arabian Nights Oasis: clusters of 2–3 palms sit on each grass patch; EarthBound: dense palm groves along the river/coast, then lone palms every 8–15 tiles inland as texture. Never plant palms in rows or even spacing except along a formal road. (`arabian-nights-oasis-fullmap.png`, `earthbound-scaraba-snes-map.webp`).
14. **Cluster geometry: triangle or L, 1–2 tiles between trunks, never a straight 3-in-a-row.** Kakkara's border and Suhalla's corner palms always stagger. (`secret-of-mana-kakkara-snes-map.webp`, `golden-sun-suhalla-gba-map.webp`).
15. **Use palm clusters to mark exits and corners.** Arabian Nights camp puts palm+jar clusters at the 4 corners of the camp; Suhalla flanks its map exits with palms. Put a 2–3 palm cluster within 3 tiles of every zone exit as a signpost. (`arabian-nights-desert-dwellers-camp-fullmap.png`, `golden-sun-suhalla-gba-map.webp`).
16. **Dense palm forest is a WALL, not decoration:** when used as boundary (Kakkara), pack trunks 1 tile apart in a 4–8 tile band and make it unwalkable; inside settlements keep palms sparse (1 per 6×6) so shadows don't clutter buildings. (`secret-of-mana-kakkara-snes-map.webp`).

## 4. Desert settlement layout (oasis_village, desert_marketplace)

17. **Hamlet scale: 3–5 buildings; town scale: 8–12; both cluster in groups of 2–3 with 3–6 tile gaps — never even grids.** Suhalla = 5 buildings around one open center; Naribwe = 5 huts; Lalivero ≈ 10 inside walls; Kaipo ≈ 7. Adjacent buildings within a group sit 2–3 tiles apart; groups sit 4–8 tiles apart. (`golden-sun-suhalla-gba-map.webp`, `golden-sun-lost-age-naribwe-gba-map.webp`, `golden-sun-lalivero-gba-map.webp`, `ff4-oasis-village-kaipo-fullmap.png`).
18. **Building footprints 4×3 to 6×5 tiles, one landmark structure 8×6+.** GBA-era houses are ~5×4 with a 1-tile door centered on the south face. Every settlement has exactly one oversized landmark: Lalivero's central tower, Isis' castle, Scaraba's hotel block, Burning Heroes' grand tent. Landmark goes at top-center or dead-center; approach to it is axial (straight 2–3 tile path from the main gate). (`golden-sun-lalivero-gba-map.webp`, `dragon-warrior-iii-isis-castle-town-nes-map.webp`, `burning-heroes-oasis-fullmap.png`).
19. **All doors face south or toward the central open space.** Universal across Suhalla, Kaipo, Naribwe, Scaraba. On our grid: place buildings so door tiles open onto the trampled-path network with at least 2 clear tiles in front. (`ff4-oasis-village-kaipo-fullmap.png`, `earthbound-scaraba-snes-map.webp`).
20. **Main streets 2–3 tiles wide, side paths 1–2, plaza 6–12 tiles across.** Tolbi's fountain plaza is ~10 tiles wide with 2–3 tile streets radiating; Scaraba's main east-west street is 3 tiles. Plazas are the widest space in the map and contain the well/fountain plus 2–3 prop clusters. (`golden-sun-tolbi-gba-map.webp`, `earthbound-scaraba-snes-map.webp`).
21. **Walled towns: wall band 1–2 tiles thick, ONE main gate (bottom-center), gate 2–3 tiles wide, wall set 3–5 tiles back from map edge.** Lalivero and Scaraba both run the wall as a rectangle with the town inside; outside the wall is empty approach sand — this sells "shelter from the desert". Buildings inside hug the wall, leaving the center open. (`golden-sun-lalivero-gba-map.webp`, `earthbound-scaraba-snes-map.webp`).
22. **Market = repeated small stall units in a loose grid beside the main street.** Scaraba lays rectangular market rugs ~2×3 tiles in two ragged rows with 1–2 tile aisles; Tolbi rings awning stalls around the plaza. For `desert_marketplace`: 6–10 stall units (rug + awning + crate/jar pile), aisles 2 tiles, one anchor fountain/landmark at the row intersection. (`earthbound-scaraba-snes-map.webp`, `golden-sun-tolbi-gba-map.webp`).
23. **Let one water channel or rivulet enter the settlement.** Kaipo threads 1-tile water channels between buildings; Suhalla has spring pools at the cliff base; Isis rings the castle with a moat-lake with palm shores. One 1-tile channel from map edge to the pond, crossed by a 2-tile bridge/plank, adds instant structure. (`ff4-oasis-village-kaipo-fullmap.png`, `dragon-warrior-iii-isis-castle-town-nes-map.webp`).
24. **Multi-screen desert wilderness between landmarks: corridor width 6–12 tiles, winding, with a landmark visible every screen.** Golden Sun's Suhalla Desert and EarthBound's dunes wind sand corridors between rock walls; travel legs between points of interest are 15–25 tiles. Applies to any desert connector areas between zones. (`golden-sun-suhalla-desert-gba-map.webp`, `earthbound-dusty-dunes-desert-snes-map.webp`).

## 5. Tent-camp composition (bedouin_camp)

25. **Camp = 5–8 tents; the canonical Arabian pattern is a QUINCUNX (X): 2 tents top row, 1 center, 2 bottom row.** Arabian Nights' Desert Dwellers' Camp is exactly this — five 4×4 golden tents, ~3–5 tiles between tent edges, banner poles filling gaps between them. Copy this pattern for the core of `bedouin_camp`. (`arabian-nights-desert-dwellers-camp-fullmap.png`).
26. **Alternative pattern: tent RING around a central pool/fire.** Burning Heroes: chief's grand tent top-center facing south, rectangular pool ~6×4 below it, 7 dome tents (3×3–4×4) ringed around the pool with all doors facing inward, N–S axis running gate→pool→grand tent. Rings = nomad/civilian camps. (`burning-heroes-oasis-fullmap.png`).
27. **ROWS + walls = military camps only.** FF6 Imperial Camp puts large tents in orthogonal rows inside stockade compartments with corner watchtowers and crate stacks; GDLeen encampments are the same. Never use row layout for the bedouin camp — rows read as army. (`ff6-imperial-camp-tents-fullmap.png`, `gdleen-bavalis-encampment-fullmap.png`).
28. **One tent is the CHIEF'S: 1.5–2× bigger, top-center, facing down the central axis.** Burning Heroes' multi-spired grand tent; Eien no Filena's central lodge among the yurts. Give it 2 banner poles flanking the entrance. (`burning-heroes-oasis-fullmap.png`, `eien-no-filena-nomad-camp-mountains-fullmap.png`).
29. **Tent footprint 3×3–4×4; door 1 tile, centered south; interiors are one oval room ~9×7 with lamps flanking the door and jars along the back wall.** Arabian Nights interior sheets show the standard: sand floor, warm lamp glow, 3–5 props total. (`arabian-nights-desert-dwellers-camp-fullmap.png`).
30. **Fire pit = the focal point of any sub-camp: center of a clearing, with seats/bedrolls RADIALLY around it at radius 2–3 tiles.** Chrono Trigger's campsite ring and Seliel's vignette both use: fire center, bedroll 2 tiles away between tent and fire, stump/log seat opposite the tent, tent mouth 2–3 tiles from the fire, facing it. Place one fire pit per 2–3 tents; the main fire sits on the camp's central axis. (`chrono-trigger-1000ad-campsite.png`, `seliel-travelers-camp-tent-fire-scene.png`, `golden-sun-tla-hesperia-settlement-nomad-camp.png`).
31. **Mark the camp boundary with LIGHT elements, not walls: woven mats/rug strips, banner poles, hitching rails, jar+palm corner clusters.** Arabian Nights runs a 1-tile woven-mat strip along the top and bottom edges of the camp rectangle and leaves the sides open with banner poles; Burning Heroes uses short fence/hitching segments (3–4 tiles) between outer tents. The camp should read as a ~20×20 rectangle you can see across. (`arabian-nights-desert-dwellers-camp-fullmap.png`, `burning-heroes-oasis-fullmap.png`).
32. **Livestock pen at the camp edge: fenced 5×4–8×6 rectangle, NE or NW corner, gate facing the tents.** Eien no Filena's sheep pen and Mikasalla's stock pens sit at the settlement edge, fence = 1-tile log posts. Kenmi: goat/camel pen with hay + trough props. (`eien-no-filena-nomad-camp-mountains-fullmap.png`, `golden-sun-tla-mikasalla-village-fullmap.png`).
33. **Rest-stop variant (2 tents): stagger them diagonally beside the trail, doors facing the trail, never side-by-side.** YS5's merchant camp: two tents offset by ~3 tiles on both axes along the path. Good for any mini-camp encounter in connector areas. (`ys5-desert-oasis-fullmap.png`).
34. **Supply dressing: crate/barrel stacks in 2×2–2×3 blocks near the chief's tent and the pen; jars in 2–4 clumps beside each tent door.** Wozz stacks supplies NE of the pond; FF6 stacks crates along tent walls. (`wozz-oasis-fullmap.png`, `ff6-imperial-camp-tents-fullmap.png`).

## 6. Farmland structure (farmland zone)

35. **Farmstead buildings cluster in ONE corner or along ONE edge — never scattered among fields.** HM SNES: house+barn+silo+coop all in the NW corner inside a fenced yard; HM MFOMT: all buildings in a row along the top edge, doors south; Stardew standard: farmhouse NE beside the road entrance. Put the Gogo farmhouse+barn cluster along the top edge with 2–3 tiles between buildings. (`harvestmoon-snes-farm-spring-fullmap.png`, `harvestmoon-mfomt-farm-spring-fullmap.png`, `stardew-standard-farm-fullmap.png`).
36. **Barn+silo are glued together (silo touching the barn's side); the silo/windmill is the farm's vertical landmark, visible over the fields.** HM SNES/GB twin silos; Sun Haven's windmill at the field corner. One tall structure per farm. (`harvestmoon-snes-farm-spring-fullmap.png`, `harvestmoon-gb-ranch-fullmap.png`, `sun-haven-farm-barn-pens-windmill-crops.jpg`).
37. **Field sizes: one working plot = 6×4 to 12×8 tiles; a big "estate" field = up to 24×14; leave 2–3 tile grass/sand margins between plots.** HM MFOMT has one huge rectangular field filling the map center; Mistria and Sun Haven break farming into several fenced 8×6-ish beds. For a playable RPG zone use 3–5 plots of 6×4–10×8 rather than one giant field. (`harvestmoon-mfomt-farm-spring-fullmap.png`, `fields-of-mistria-farmyard-house-well-croprows.jpg`, `sun-haven-farm-barn-pens-windmill-crops.jpg`).
38. **Crop-row geometry: rows run parallel, 1 tile of crop + 1 tile of dirt gap, 3–6 rows per plot, rows 4–8 tiles long; all rows in one plot share direction; adjacent plots may rotate 90°.** Mistria's turnip beds (3 rows × 4), Sun Haven's lavender strips (2-row strips with stone walkway between), Roots of Pacha's dense bounded blocks. Different crop type per plot = instant variety. (`fields-of-mistria-farmyard-house-well-croprows.jpg`, `sun-haven-farm-barn-pens-windmill-crops.jpg`, `roots-of-pacha-crops-irrigation-channels.jpg`).
39. **Fences: 1-tile wood/log fence around each crop plot and pen, with a 1–2 tile gate gap facing the farmhouse; a perimeter fence around the WHOLE farm only if the zone is the farm.** HM GB rings the entire ranch in log fence with one gate; Mistria fences individual beds; Route 117 fences its flower beds beside the path. Fence corners always closed; gates aligned with paths. (`harvestmoon-gb-ranch-fullmap.png`, `fields-of-mistria-winter-fenced-crop-beds.jpg`, `pokemon-rubysapphire-route117-daycare-flowerfields-fullmap.png`).
40. **Irrigation channels: 1 tile wide, running along plot EDGES (never through the middle of a plot), fed from the pond/river, with a waterwheel or wooden gate prop at each corner/junction, and 1–2 tile plank crossings where paths cross them.** Roots of Pacha is the model: blue channels outline the crop blocks and loop back to the source. Perfect Arabian falaj/qanat flavor for Gogo: run a channel from the oasis pond along the field edges. (`roots-of-pacha-crops-irrigation-channels.jpg`).
41. **Water source placement: pond or well within 6 tiles of the crop plots, between house and fields.** HM SNES puts the well in the yard beside the field; Mistria's well sits beside the house; Pacha's channels all originate at the water. (`harvestmoon-snes-farm-spring-fullmap.png`, `fields-of-mistria-farmyard-house-well-croprows.jpg`).
42. **Animal pen: fenced 8×6–12×8 grass rectangle adjacent to the barn (sharing the barn's south or east face), with hay feeders along the fence line and a water trough; 2–4 animals.** Sun Haven's pen with hay bales in feeders is the model; Mistria lets chickens roam the open yard between house and beds. (`sun-haven-farm-barn-pens-windmill-crops.jpg`, `fields-of-mistria-farmyard-house-well-croprows.jpg`).
43. **Path logic: one 2-tile dirt path from the zone entrance to the farmhouse door, branching 1-tile paths to barn, pen gate, and each plot gate; paths never cut through crops.** HM GB's gate→barn junction path; Mistria's stepping-stone spurs. (`harvestmoon-gb-ranch-fullmap.png`, `fields-of-mistria-farmstead-barn-fences.jpg`).
44. **A farm plaza/social anchor: small 4×4–6×6 paved or trampled area with fire/seating/beehive props where paths meet — between the plots, not inside them.** Roots of Pacha centers its farmland on a stone plaza with a fire and benches; gives NPCs a place to stand. (`roots-of-pacha-crops-irrigation-channels.jpg`).
45. **Desert-farm dressing:** replace grass with hardy scrub: date-palm orchard block (palms in a fenced 8×6 grid, 2-tile spacing — the ONE place palms may be regular), wheat plots in golden tone, grass only inside the irrigation halo. Wozz shows fenced crop squares sitting directly on the oasis grass ring — crops live inside the green blob, buildings on the sand outside it. (`wozz-oasis-fullmap.png`, `sun-haven-decorated-farm-orchard-croprows.jpg`).

## 7. Numbers cheat-sheet (defaults when a rule doesn't say otherwise)

46. Main road 2–3 tiles; side path 1–2; plaza 6–12 across; corridor in wilderness 6–12.
47. House 4×3–6×5; landmark 8×6+; tent 3×3–4×4; chief tent ~6×5; stall 2×3.
48. Gaps: buildings in a group 2–3 tiles; groups 4–8; tents 3–5; plots 2–3.
49. Water: village pond 4×3–8×6; grass halo 1–2 tiles; channel 1 tile; bridge/plank 2 tiles.
50. Boundary band 2–6 tiles thick; exits 2–3 tiles wide, marked by palm/totem/banner cluster within 3 tiles.
51. Interest density: 1 prop cluster per 8–15 tiles of open ground; 1 ground decal per 4×4; fire pit radius: seats at 2–3 tiles.
